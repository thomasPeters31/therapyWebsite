Frequently asked questions

What happens in a counselling session?

We talk about whatever feels important to you. There is no script or pressure, just a safe space to explore thoughts, feelings, and patterns.

How many sessions will I need?

The number varies. Some people come for 6 to 12 sessions, others choose longer-term support. We review goals regularly.

Is what I share confidential?

Everything is confidential with rare exceptions for serious risk of harm or legal requirements. Wherever possible this is discussed with you first.
