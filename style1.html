About me

Hello, I am Natasha, though most people call me Tasha. Before training as a counsellor, I spent 24 years as a teacher, supporting children and young people aged 5 to 18 through academic pressure, emotional challenges, and major life changes.

I now work in schools and have a private practice with children and adults in short and long-term therapy. I believe in the quiet power of being truly listened to. Therapy does not have to be about being fixed. Sometimes it is about having someone alongside you, helping you slow down and reconnect with your own resources.

Professional background

Training and membership Registered member of the BACP with diplomas in Therapeutic Counselling (CPCAB). Currently completing a Level 5 diploma in Psychotherapeutic Counselling.
Experience and care Extensive experience supporting neurodivergent children and adults, including autism, ADHD, sensory differences, and emotional regulation challenges.

How I work

My approach is integrative, holistic, and person-centred. I do not try to fit you into one model of therapy. I work with you as a whole person, tailoring support to your unique experiences and needs.

CBT-informed tools Transactional Analysis Internal Family Systems Creative and expressive methods Trauma-informed practice Psychodynamic therapy Gestalt therapy Inner child work Play therapy
Adults and young people Anxiety, depression, stress, grief, divorce, communication challenges, low self-esteem, peri-menopause, neurodiversity, and perfectionism.
Children Support with anxiety, low mood, friendship issues, school challenges, loss, emotional regulation, and family changes through creative and play-based work.
Taking the first step

You can show up feeling unsure, overwhelmed, or quiet. There is no pressure to have the right words. A free 15-minute introductory call is available.

Fees Sessions are 65 pounds per therapeutic hour (50 minutes). 180 pounds when booked as a block of three or more. Concessions available for low-waged earners and student counsellors.
