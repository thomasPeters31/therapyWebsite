Frequently asked questions

If you have a question that is not covered here, please reach out and I will be happy to help.

What happens in a first session? We will talk through what brings you to therapy, what you want from sessions, and how I can support you. There is no pressure to share anything you are not ready to discuss.
Do you offer a free introductory call? Yes. I offer a free 15-minute call so you can ask questions and see if we are a good fit before booking.
How long are sessions? Sessions are 50 minutes. We will agree on a frequency that suits you, often weekly or fortnightly.
Do you work with children and young people? Yes. I support children, young people, and adults, adapting the work to their age and needs.
Where do sessions take place? In-person sessions are available in Ashford, Kent, and I also offer online video or telephone sessions.
What are your fees? Sessions are 65 pounds per therapeutic hour. Blocks of three or more are 180 pounds. Concessions are available for low-waged earners and student counsellors.
Counselling HQ is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis, contact your GP, call 999 if in immediate danger, or reach Samaritans on 116 123.
