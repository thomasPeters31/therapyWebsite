Frequently asked questions

If you have a question that is not covered here, please reach out and I will be happy to help.

What happens in a counselling session? We talk about whatever feels important to you. There is no script or pressure, just a safe space to explore thoughts, feelings, experiences, behaviours, and patterns at your pace.
What will happen in our free introductory call? The free introductory call is a chance to talk through what has brought you to counselling and what support you are looking for. You are welcome to ask any questions you may have. If you wish to proceed with therapy, I will email you a short questionnaire and our counselling agreement, so expectations and confidentiality are clear.
What will happen in the first session? In the first session, whether by phone, online, or in person, we will take time to get to know each other. You are welcome to ask any questions and get a sense of whether I feel like the right fit for you.

The first session will be a little different as I will complete an assessment. We will talk about what has brought you to counselling, a little about your background, and what you hope to gain from our work together. We will also go through our counselling agreement, so expectations, boundaries, and confidentiality are clear. We will discuss your questionnaire so I can understand and have a clear idea of what you want, need, and how we can tailor our work to achieve your goals.

There is no pressure or obligation to continue. This first session is simply a chance for us to explore whether working together feels right for you.

How many sessions will I need? The number of sessions varies for each individual. Some people come for a short time (6-12 sessions), while others choose longer-term support. We will regularly review your goals together as we progress and go at a pace that suits you.
Can I end counselling at any time? Yes, you are free to end our counselling whenever you feel ready. Where possible, it is advised that we plan an ending session together, which brings our work to a gentle close.
How often will the sessions occur? Weekly sessions often work best, or fortnightly if this is your preference. Regularly meeting supports continuity and depth in our work together. We will arrange what suits you best depending on your wants, needs, and life challenges.
When and how do I pay for sessions? Payment for sessions will be expected via cash or bank transfer before each session.
Is what I share completely confidential? Everything you share in counselling is treated with care and kept confidential. There are a few rare situations where I may need to share information, such as if there is a serious risk of harm to you or someone else, concerns about the safety of a child or vulnerable adult, or where the law requires it (for example, in relation to terrorism, money laundering, or a court order). Wherever possible, this would always be discussed with you first.
Counselling HQ is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis, contact your GP, call 999 if in immediate danger, or reach Samaritans on 116 123.
