Frequently asked questions

What happens in the first session?

We take time to get to know each other. I complete a short assessment, we discuss what brought you to counselling, and we review the counselling agreement so expectations and confidentiality are clear.

Can I end counselling at any time?

Yes. You are free to end counselling whenever you feel ready. Where possible we plan an ending session together to bring the work to a gentle close.

How often will sessions occur?

Weekly sessions often work best, or fortnightly if that is your preference. We will arrange what suits you depending on your needs.
