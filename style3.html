Taking the first step can feel hard.

There is no pressure to have the right words. You can show up feeling unsure, overwhelmed, or quiet — all of you is welcome. I offer a free 15-minute introductory call to explore working together.

Policies & ethics

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are still charged. As a member of the BACP, I commit to high ethical standards, ongoing training, and supervision.

Signposting

Counselling HQ is not an emergency service. In crisis, contact your GP, call 999 if in immediate danger, or reach Samaritans on 116 123 (24/7).
